Premix & Frozen Premix medications

Pharmaceutical labeling, kept in lockstep.

GG managed development, review, approval and version control of labeling for Premix and Frozen Premix medications, keeping core and regional documents consistent.

Labeling hierarchy

One core, two regions.

Select a document to see its role.

TPL · Target Product Labeling

The labeling target set early in development. It defines the claims and content the product is expected to support.

CCDS · Company Core Data Sheet

The company's core safety and product information. Every regional label is checked against it, and any deviation is documented and justified.

USPI · US Prescribing Information

Aligned with the FDA Physician Labeling Rule format under 21 CFR 201.56 and 201.57.

EU SmPC · Summary of Product Characteristics

Aligned with the EMA QRD template, with regional deviations from the CCDS documented.

Consistency & control

Every change traceable

  • Reviewed labeling for accuracy and consistency between CCDS and regional documents
  • Maintained controlled records of labeling changes
  • Communicated approved updates at implementation
  • Documented and justified regional deviations
Cross-functional coordination

From package insert to carton

  • Package inserts, container labels and cartons
  • Regulatory, Medical, Supply Chain and Artwork teams
  • Storage, thawing and handling statements specific to frozen premix presentations
