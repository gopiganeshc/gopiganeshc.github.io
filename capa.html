The remediation loop

Six stages, one program.

Step through how each nonconformance moved from containment to closure.

Contain

Immediate measures to protect customers and supply.

  • Temporary Authorized Labeling
  • Controlled distribution strategies
  • No product shortages across global markets

Assess

Product impact assessments and Health Hazard Evaluations.

  • Health Hazard Evaluations (HHE)
  • Reportability assessment with Complaint Handling and PMS
  • Risk-based remediation strategy

Investigate

Systemic causes in labeling controls, packaging, artwork, accessories and process execution.

  • 5-Why and Fishbone analysis
  • Failure mode assessment and complaint trending
  • DHR reviews

Correct

Labeling and packaging solutions that reached compliance without redesign.

  • No packaging redesign, tooling or line changes
  • Less validation and lower implementation cost
  • Faster deployment across sites

Transfer

Design transfer and manufacturing implementation.

  • Change controls, DHF/DMR and BOM updates
  • Labeling specifications and work instructions
  • V&V assessments, FOC reviews and operator training

Govern

Program governance to close high-priority actions on time.

  • Integrated plans and CAPA milestone tracking
  • KPI dashboards and risk registers
  • Executive reviews backed by risk-benefit analysis
