How the program ran

Strategy, readiness, execution.

Phase 1 · Strategy & governance

Regulation translated into business requirements

  • Interpreted EU MDR 2017/745 Annex I GSPR, including Chapter III Section 23
  • Translated MDCG guidance, harmonized standards and Notified Body expectations
  • Risk-based product prioritization tied to technical documentation timelines
  • Recurring governance reviews across ten functions
Phase 2 · Gap assessment & readiness

Every product type, assessed

  • Combination products, active and non-active devices
  • Sterile systems and software-enabled products
  • Targeted EU MDR, UDI and labeling training
  • Stage-gate governance with independent expert reviews
Phase 3 · Remediation execution

From data collection to production

  • Impact assessments and technical content generation
  • End-to-end lifecycle shown in the ring above
  • Traceability across Technical Documentation, DHF/DMR and artwork
  • Manufacturing deployment and post-release control
