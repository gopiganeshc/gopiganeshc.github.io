Signature program

De Novo for a novel dialysis device.

No predicate meant building the case from first principles. Step through the five stages.

Classification strategy

Built the risk-based classification strategy and device classification rationale for a novel dialysis device with no legally marketed predicate.

  • Benefit-risk assessment and probable benefits versus risks analysis
  • Proposed special controls
  • Classification rationale under 21 CFR 860 Subpart D

Pre-Submission

Integrated FDA Pre-Sub feedback into the testing strategy and documentation plan.

  • Q-Sub planning and question framing
  • Feedback mapped to evidence gaps
  • Testing strategy adjusted before execution

Evidence package

Coordinated R&D, Clinical, Quality, Biocompatibility and Regulatory to assemble reasonable assurance of safety and effectiveness.

  • Performance and biocompatibility evidence
  • Usability and sterilization evidence
  • Labeling evidence and technical documentation

Deficiency response

Managed deficiency responses as the liaison between engineering teams and FDA reviewers.

  • Scientific responses to Additional Information requests
  • Engineering input translated for reviewers
  • Timelines protected through review

Grant & launch

Carried the program through classification grant into commercialization readiness.

  • Classification grant
  • Commercialization readiness
  • Special controls built into downstream processes
